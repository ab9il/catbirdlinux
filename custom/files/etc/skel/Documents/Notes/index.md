### You (yes, YOU) should build your second brain!
 - Use markdown format.
 - Interlink your notes with other notes.
 - Interlink other media.
 - Make use of check boxes and folds.
 - Back your notes up on a separate device!

### Obsidian is an excellent app for managing your knowledge.
 - Use markdown format.
 - Easy CSS based fonts and colors.
 - Create notes and slide presentations.
 - Sophisticated tools for analysis.
 - Customize with hundreds of available plugins.
